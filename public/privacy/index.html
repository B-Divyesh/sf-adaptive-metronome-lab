Privacy

Tempo Lab works without an account. Saved drills and practice attempts are stored in this browser on this device.

What stays in this browser

  • Named drills and their tempo settings
  • Practice dates, durations, and completion status
  • Sound, visual, and vibration choices

Tempo Lab does not request microphone access or record your playing.

Sharing and exports

A share link contains a drill name and settings. It does not include your practice log or a device identifier.

CSV and JSON files are created in your browser. You choose where to save or send them.

Control and deletion

You can delete individual drills and practice attempts in the app. Clearing this site’s browser data removes its stored data.

Export a JSON backup before clearing browser data if you want to keep your records.

Hosting

Tempo Lab has no third-party runtime scripts. The host may keep short-lived access logs for security and reliability.

Contact

Use the contact channel at sociobot.in for privacy questions.
